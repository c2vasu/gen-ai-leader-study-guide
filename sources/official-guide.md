# Exam Outline Summary

A paraphrased outline (not a copy) of the official **Generative AI Leader** exam guide, used to structure this repo. For the authoritative wording, read the original:

- Exam guide: https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/generative_ai_leader_exam_guide_english.pdf
- Study guide: https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/generative_ai_leader_study_guide_english.pdf

## Section 1: Fundamentals of gen AI (~30%)
- 1.1 Core AI terms (AI, NLP, ML, foundation models, LLMs, diffusion models, prompting); supervised vs. unsupervised vs. reinforcement learning; ML lifecycle stages and Google Cloud tools; choosing foundation models on business factors; use cases for creating, summarizing, discovering and automating.
- 1.2 Data quality and accessibility; structured vs. unstructured; labeled vs. unlabeled; business implications.
- 1.3 Gen AI landscape layers: infrastructure, models, platforms, agents, applications.
- 1.4 Strengths and use cases of Gemini, Gemma, Imagen and Veo.

## Section 2: Google Cloud's gen AI offerings (~35%)
- 2.1 Google Cloud's strengths: AI-first, enterprise-ready and responsible, integrated ecosystem, open approach, AI-optimized infrastructure; data control; democratized development tools.
- 2.2 Prebuilt offerings: Gemini app / Gemini Advanced, Gemini Enterprise, Gemini for Google Workspace.
- 2.3 Customer experience: external search offerings and the customer experience suite.
- 2.4 Developer tools: Agent Platform features, RAG offerings, custom agent building.
- 2.5 Agent tooling (extensions, functions, data stores, plugins); relevant services and pre-built APIs; Agent Studio vs. Google AI Studio.

## Section 3: Techniques to improve gen AI model output (~20%)
- 3.1 Limitations (data dependence, knowledge cutoff, bias, hallucinations, edge cases); grounding, RAG, prompting, fine-tuning, HITL; monitoring, drift and versioning.
- 3.2 Prompt engineering: zero/one/few-shot, role, chaining, chain-of-thought, ReAct.
- 3.3 Grounding with first-party, third-party and world data; RAG; Google grounding offerings; temperature, top-p, token limits, safety settings, output length.

## Section 4: Business strategies for a successful gen AI solution (~15%)
- 4.1 Solution types, need factors, solution selection, integration steps, measuring impact.
- 4.2 Security across the ML lifecycle; Secure AI Framework (SAIF); Google Cloud security tools.
- 4.3 Responsible AI and transparency; privacy, anonymization and pseudonymization; data quality, bias and fairness; accountability and explainability.
